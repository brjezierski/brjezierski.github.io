Powered by fine-tuned OPUS-MT models, one per direction (Silesian→Polish, Polish→Silesian) trained on Silesian-Polish Wikipedia. This is a research demo, so check important translations. The first request after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the model wakes up.

More info

Silesian is a regional language spoken by around half a million people, yet it is poorly supported by existing translation systems. I fine-tuned the multilingual Slavic OPUS-MT model to build one model per direction.

The training data comes from Silesian and Polish Wikipedia: I matched articles through shared titles, split them into sentences and aligned them with hunalign, using a Polish-Silesian dictionary of about 21,000 entries scraped from an online Silesian dictionary. Each model was then fine-tuned for 10 epochs on roughly 60,000 sentence pairs.

I also released a 100-sentence Silesian-Polish benchmark. On it, the models reach chrF++ 53–54 in both directions.
